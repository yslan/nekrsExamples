  All four errors are max |f - c| against the ANALYTIC field, sampled
  INSIDE every triangle -- not at the nodes, where the answer is trivially
  exact.  The four columns separate the two error sources:
    flat      flat marching-cubes triangles
    PN recon  curved using ONLY the stored payload (positions + normals),
              with no further access to the element.  THE CONSTRAINT CASE.
    projected subdivide + Newton on the interpolant (needs element access):
              the best the pipeline can do, kept as the reference
    floor     distance from the INTERPOLANT's level set to the analytic one
              -- the discretisation error, which no surface method can beat

  Reading it: PN reconstruction roughly halves the flat error at ~5 floats
  per vertex and zero element access.  Projection does 7-11x better but
  needs the element data.  Both remain far above the interpolation floor on
  'tunnel' (7.9e-08), so on that case the limit is the RECONSTRUCTION, not
  the discretisation -- there is room for a richer payload.  On 'gyroid' the
  floor is 4.1e-04 and the field is barely resolved at N=8, so refining the
  surface matters less than raising N.

    tunnel   MC case 13: the tunnel
             alternating corner signs; face and interior ambiguity interact. Sign-based MC needs MC33's subcase tables; we evaluate the polynomial.
    gyroid   gyroid: many sheets, high genus
             a triply-periodic minimal surface: several disconnected sheets in one element and the largest number of ambiguous cells of any case here.
    tgv      Taylor-Green vortex: Q-criterion
             a REAL flow field, not a manufactured one: the Q-criterion of the standard Taylor-Green initial condition on one big undeformed element spanning the whole [0,2pi]^3 cell. Eight vortex tubes meet at the cell centre, so the isosurface has genuine flow topology; at N=16 one element resolves Q to 3.4e-07.
    neck     neck: a topology change in progress
             two lobes at the moment of merging; |grad f| ~ 2e-2 at the neck, so this is the sqrt(eps) tangency of section 2 and the case where gradient projection must be guarded.
